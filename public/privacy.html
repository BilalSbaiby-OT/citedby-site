Privacy

The public pages set no cookies and run no third-party scripts. One cookie exists: if you sign in to an account, we set ci_session. It keeps you signed in for 30 days, is strictly necessary for that, is HttpOnly, and is never used for tracking or advertising. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, time) for security and to count visits; they are kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

If you email us or buy the audit, we use your shop URL and email address only to deliver the audit and answer you. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card details; we never see them. We count anonymous events per day and language (check started, check completed, email submitted, Fix Pack link clicked), with no IP address or identifier. If you ask for the email mini-audit, we store your email address, shop address, country, language and niche, and send a confirmation email first (double opt-in). We write to you only about that audit and one follow-up, delete unconfirmed requests after 7 days and all requests after 12 months, and you can delete yours at any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails. Our server logs your IP address briefly to limit abuse. To access or delete your data, write to hello@citedby.eu.

If you save a count to an account, we keep your email address, the language you chose, your shop name and address, the saved counts (which assistants named your shop, which rivals they named) and, if you subscribe, the Stripe subscription id and plan. We send you a sign-in link by email; it works once and for 15 minutes, and we store it only as a hash. We keep a mail log of what we send you, which holds the address and subject but not the sign-in link. Stripe keeps payment and invoice records on its own side.

You can export your account data from your dashboard and delete your account there. Deleting cancels any active subscription first, then removes the account, shop, saved counts and recount reports, sign-in links, and your address from the mail log. The free check also stores the shop address and the count it produced in our check queue; write to hello@citedby.eu to have those removed.

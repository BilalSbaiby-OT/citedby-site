Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party scripts. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, time) for security and to count visits; they are kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

If you email us or buy the audit, we use your shop URL and email address only to deliver the audit and answer you. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card details; we never see them. If you use the free check at /check, your shop address is fetched once to run the check and the result is not stored. We count anonymous events per day and language (check started, check completed, email submitted, Fix Pack link clicked), with no IP address or identifier. If you ask for the email mini-audit, we store your email address, shop address, country, language and niche, and send a confirmation email first (double opt-in). We write to you only about that audit and one follow-up, delete unconfirmed requests after 7 days and all requests after 12 months, and you can delete yours at any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails. Our server logs your IP address briefly to limit abuse. To access or delete your data, write to hello@citedby.eu.

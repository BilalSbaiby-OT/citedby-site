AI visibility audit for an online shop

CitedBy is an AI visibility audit for online shops, not a citation tool: we ask AI assistants the questions your buyers ask and report whether your shop is named. This page explains what such an audit (also called a GEO or AEO audit) covers and how to run a basic one yourself.

Run the free check (about 10 seconds, no email).

What an AI visibility audit checks

  1. Are you named? Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions a buyer asks about your category in your country and language, for example "best organic coffee beans to buy in Ireland". Note whether your shop is named or linked.
  2. Can AI crawlers read your shop? Open yourshop.com/robots.txt. GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot must not be disallowed. Then view the page source of a product page: if the price, name and description are missing from the HTML, many crawlers cannot see them.
  3. Does the page answer the question in plain text? Assistants quote short, direct passages: what the product is, who it suits, price, delivery, returns.
  4. Is there structured data? Product, Offer and FAQ markup (JSON-LD) tell machines which number is the price and which text is the answer.
  5. Are you mentioned elsewhere? Assistants lean on pages that compare and review shops. Check which sites they link when they answer, and whether you appear there.
  6. Do AI assistants send visitors? In Google Analytics 4, look at the traffic sources for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com.

Run it twice

AI answers change between runs. Ask each question twice, logged out, and count a shop as reliably named only if it appears both times. That is how CitedBy counts, and every report lists the sites linked in each try.

Questions

Are GEO, AEO and AI visibility audits the same thing?

In practice yes. GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization) and AI visibility all describe the work of being named in the answers AI assistants give.

Is CitedBy a tool for academic citations?

No. CitedBy is for online shops. It does not format or check academic references.

Is the check free?

Yes. The check needs no email. You pay only if you want the fixes done.

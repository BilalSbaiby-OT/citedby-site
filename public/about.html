About CitedBy

CitedBy is a small independent service run from Spain. We check what AI assistants answer when a buyer asks about an online shop's category, and we fix what keeps a shop out of the answer.

How we count

We ask each assistant through its official API the questions a buyer would ask, in the buyer's language, each twice. Every assistant is asked with web search on, and the report names each one and the date. A shop counts as named when its name appears in the answer text. Answers vary between runs, so every audit lists the sites linked in each try, and you can check our count yourself.

What we have measured

Our data pages list who ChatGPT linked, question by question, with the method and the raw CSV. You can read a sample mini-audit before you ask for one.

How we earn money

We sell the fixes: the Fix Pack at €390, paid once, and CitedBy Monthly at €149 per month after your day-30 recount. The audit is free. See the refund conditions and the terms.

About CitedBy

CitedBy is a small independent service run from Spain. We check whether ChatGPT names online shops when a buyer asks about their category, and we fix what keeps a shop out of the answer.

How we count

We ask ChatGPT, logged out with web search on, the questions a buyer would ask, in the buyer's language, each twice. A shop counts as named when its name appears in the answer text. Answers vary between runs, so every audit lists the sites ChatGPT linked in each try, and you can check our count yourself.

What we have measured

127 ChatGPT answers to 64 buyer questions in 5 EU niches, on 13 and 14 Oct 2026. The study has the method and the raw CSV, and the data pages list who got linked by question. You can read a sample mini-audit before you ask for one.

How we earn money

We sell the fixes: the Fix Pack at €390, paid once, and CitedBy Monthly at €149 per month after your day-30 recount. The audit is free. See the refund conditions and the terms.

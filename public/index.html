No. Nobody controls what an assistant answers. We guarantee the work: changes live (or files delivered) within 7 days, and if your count doesn't rise at the day-30 re-check, we keep working another 30 days at no charge.

Do you need access to my shop?

For the Fix Pack, yes: a Shopify collaborator request or a CMS login, so we can make the changes. If you prefer, we send every change as ready-to-paste files and you apply them.

Trackers tell you whether you show up. They don't rewrite your pages, fix wrong facts or ask the guides ChatGPT reads to list you. That work is what you pay us for.

Counting rule: a shop counts as named when its name or its domain appears in the answer text or in the links. One shop is one shop however it is written: "Café du Jour" and fr.cafedujour.be count together. "Named in 1 of 2" is never rounded up to "recommended".

2. What the Fix Pack report adds

  • 40 buyer questions, each asked twice, instead of 8.
  • The same count on every assistant we can measure that day, each with its own number.
  • A competitor map: who is named, who is linked, which pages were cited.
  • The changes made on your shop, or as ready-to-paste files.
  • The day-30 recount, before and after, with the raw answers.

Every audit also checks the technical basics on your homepage: whether robots.txt lets AI search crawlers in, whether product text is in the raw HTML, and whether the page says who you are. We check them because a failure would block everything else, not because passing them gets you named.

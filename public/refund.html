Refunds and guarantees

These are the same two guarantees shown next to the price on the home page.

1. Late changes, full refund

If you give us access (a Shopify collaborator request or a CMS login), the changes are live within 7 days. If you choose ready-to-paste files, you get them within 7 days. If we miss either date, we refund the €390.

The 7 days start the day you give us access, or the day you tell us you want the files. If we miss the date, the refund is automatic: you do not have to ask.

2. No rise at day 30, 30 more days free

At day 30 we ask the same questions again, each twice, and count the same way as in your first audit. If your shop is named in no more answers than before, we keep working for another 30 days at no charge.

Full month

We do not promise rankings, traffic or sales. Nobody controls what an assistant answers.

How a refund is paid

We refund to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show.

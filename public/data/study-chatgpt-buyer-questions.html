127 ChatGPT answers to 64 buyer questions in 5 EU niches: who gets linked

We asked ChatGPT 64 buyer questions about tea, coffee, mattresses, supplements and beauty supplements in France, Belgium and Ireland, twice each (127 answers, 13 Oct 2026 to 14 Oct 2026). In every niche one or two shops dominate: the most-linked shop appears in 38% to 88% of answers, and the top three take 26% to 50% of all shop links.

What the numbers say

  • MeasuredLinking is concentrated. The most-linked shop appears in 38% to 88% of a cell's answers; the top three shops take 26% to 50% of shop links.
  • MeasuredA single answer is a poor guide. Two runs of the same question share only 29% to 54% of the shop sites they link, on average per cell.
  • MeasuredLinks go to shops' own sites: 482 of 509 links across the eight cells (95%). Department stores, maps and review sites take the rest.
  • MeasuredTechnical hygiene does not separate linked shops from unlinked ones. In the four cells with a comparison group, 23 most-linked shop sites against 69 shops never linked: Organization schema 12 vs 45; Product schema 15 vs 34; OpenAI's search crawler not blocked 23 vs 66; page text in the raw HTML 21 vs 61. Small samples: they show these traits do not split the groups, not that they are useless.
  • MeasuredChatGPT linked 10 domains that have no DNS address, in 5 of the 8 cells, 18 times in total. They are excluded from the rankings. One of them was linked six times in a single cell.
  • Not tested yetWhether published facts (starting price, delivery time, subscription terms), reviews or third-party mentions explain who gets linked. That needs a linked-versus-unlinked comparison on those traits; it is the next measurement round.

Method and limits

  • Engine: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), logged out, web search on, temporary chat, connection from Spain.
  • Questions: 64 buyer questions in French, Dutch or English (8 per cell), each asked twice in fresh chats. 127 complete answers kept; failed runs are dropped.
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count names in running text.
  • Coverage: 5 niches, 3 countries, 3 languages. 127 answers is a small sample: read the percentages as counts, not as market share.
  • Limits: one engine, one run location, runs from 13 Oct 2026 to 14 Oct 2026; answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Site names come from ChatGPT's output. DNS and technical checks ran on 14 Oct 2026.
  • Raw data: every answer is stored with its timestamp. Pooled CSV: download.

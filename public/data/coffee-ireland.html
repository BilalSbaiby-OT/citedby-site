What the named shops have in common

Ranked by strength of evidence. These are findings about 15 answers, not causes.

  • MeasuredTwo runs of the same question do not name the same shops: average overlap is 54%. One answer tells you little; count several runs.
  • MeasuredLinks almost always go to the shop's own site: 69 of 69 links. Third-party sites (department stores, maps, review sites) account for 0 links.
  • MeasuredTechnical traits do not separate the named shops from the rest. Of the 6 most-linked sites, 3 have Organization schema, 6 let OpenAI's search crawler in and 6 show their text in the raw HTML; among 17 shops in the same category that were never linked, it is 13, 17 and 12 (same order).
  • MeasuredChatGPT linked 1 domain(s) with no DNS address (belllanecoffee.com (1x), checked 14 Oct 2026): a buyer who clicks gets an error. These domains are excluded from the table above.
  • Not tested yetFacts shown on the page (starting price, delivery time, free-shipping threshold, subscription terms), reviews and mentions in third-party guides: we have not yet compared them between named and unnamed shops. That is for the next measurement rounds.

Method and limits

  • Engine: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), logged out, web search on, temporary chat.
  • Questions: 8 buyer questions written in English, each asked 2 times in a fresh chat (15 answers kept, 13 Oct 2026).
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count a name in running text, and we never round “linked in 1 of 2 runs” up to “recommended”.
  • Limits: one engine, one run location (connection from Spain), answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Site names are ChatGPT's output, not verified by us.
  • Raw data: every answer is kept with its timestamp; the table is downloadable as CSV.

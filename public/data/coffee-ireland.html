What the named shops have in common

Ranked by strength of evidence. These are findings about 15 answers, not causes.

  • MeasuredTwo runs of the same question share on average 54% of the shops they link: count several runs rather than trusting one answer.
  • MeasuredAll 68 links go to the shops' own sites.
  • MeasuredTechnical traits do not separate the named shops from the rest. The 6 most-linked sites we could check against 17 shops in the same category that were never linked: Organization schema 3 of 6 vs 13 of 17; OpenAI's search crawler allowed 6 of 6 vs 17 of 17; page text in the raw HTML 6 of 6 vs 12 of 17.
  • MeasuredChatGPT linked 2 domains with no working page (belllanecoffee.com (1x), cloudpickercoffee.com (1x), checked 14 Oct 2026): a buyer who clicks gets an error or an unrelated site. They are excluded from the table above.
  • Not tested yetFacts shown on the page (starting price, delivery time, free-shipping threshold, subscription terms), reviews and mentions in third-party guides: we have not yet compared them between named and unnamed shops. That is for the next measurement rounds.

Method and limits

  • Engine: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), logged out, web search on, temporary chat.
  • Questions: 8 buyer questions written in English, each asked 2 times in a fresh chat (15 answers kept, 13 Oct 2026). 1 run dropped (technical failure): 15 answers kept out of 16 planned.
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count a name in running text, and we never round “linked in 1 of 2 runs” up to “recommended”.
  • Limits: one engine, one run location (connection from Spain), answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Domains are as ChatGPT linked them; on 14 Oct 2026 we checked which ones load.
  • Raw data: every answer is kept with its timestamp; the table is downloadable as CSV.
